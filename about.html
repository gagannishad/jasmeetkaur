My Story

Learning in Public: Building Resilience Through Everyday Systems

Starting out on YouTube back in September 2025 wasn't planned. I placed my phone on a windowsill and just began recording. For six months, I balanced school, exams, and making daily videos, putting in effort every single day even when energy ran low.

When setbacks occurred and my main channel lost monetization after reaching 14,000+ subscribers, I realized that sitting back and feeling defeated changes nothing. I choose to start over from zero on a new channel, commit to daily action, and prove that consistency beats circumstance every time.

My Content on Self-Improvement, Unscripted Talk, Advice to Teenagers, Personal Growth & Practical Guidance
My Principle Action Over Theory, Take Action, Keep Improving & Becoming Best Version of Myself

Direction

Purpose & Vision

My Purpose

To show teenagers that real growth happens through daily action. I want to replace overthinking with honest systems, disciplined routines, and the courage to start even when feeling unprepared.

My Vision

To build a genuine community of young creators and students who treat comparison as motivation, stick to their craft without excuses, and build independent, purposeful lives.

The Philosophy

What Governs This Platform

Reduce The Time Gap

The gap between thinking and doing should be as small as possible. Stop waiting until everything feels perfect. Start now, learn from what happens, and move forward.

Immediate Action

Love The Process

Don’t postpone your happiness until you reach the next milestone. Growth takes time. Keep showing up, keep learning, and find meaning in the everyday work that moves you forward.

Sustainable Growth

Action Over Analysis

Overthinking can keep you stuck. You discover what works by trying, learning from the results, and taking the next step instead of endlessly thinking about the perfect one.
